POKEMON FETTUCCINE VERSION - PLAYTEST 10
========================================

Thanks for testing! This one has a new 8th Gym Leader: Joe Q, in his
BATTLE DUDS.


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
1. You need a clean US Pokemon Emerald ROM (not included).
   It should match:
     CRC32: 1F1C08FB
     SHA-1: f3ae088181bf583e55daf962a92bb46f4f1d07b7
2. Apply fettuccine-playtest-10.bps to it. Either of these works:
     - Rom Patcher JS (in your browser): https://www.marcrobledo.com/RomPatcher.js/
     - Floating IPS (Flips)
3. Play the patched ROM in mGBA or any GBA emulator.

Your Playtest 9 save should keep working: give the new ROM the same
file name as before (so the save file matches) or copy the .sav across.
Use an in-game save, not a save state from an older playtest.


NEW SINCE PLAYTEST 9
--------------------
Sugopolis Gym: Joe Q is the new Gym Leader
- Juan is gone. Joe Q, the talent scout you keep running into, runs
  the Sugopolis Gym. Turns out he's MIKE's big brother, and he taught
  Mike everything.
- Before you battle, he changes into his BATTLE DUDS. After the
  battle, he changes back.
- His team has a new member: a Palafin in its superhero Hero Form
  takes Primarina's place. The rest of his team is the same.
- Mike, the gym sign, the gym trainers, a few Sugopolis and
  Mostaccideep townspeople, and Joe Q's PokeNav calls all have new
  lines to match.
- Joe Q now has two PokeNav entries: his scout one and a Gym Leader
  one (for rematches).


PLEASE TRY
----------
- Talk to Joe Q at the top of the Sugopolis Gym. Watch him change
  into his BATTLE DUDS, beat him, and watch him change back after
  you get the badge, the TM and the PokeNav message.
- Check that his Palafin shows up as the muscly Hero Form, not the
  small dolphin.
- Talk to him again after the battle, and after leaving and coming
  back.
- If you can, lose to him once, then come back and try again.
- If you reach rematches, try one (with 2+ Pokemon, and once with
  only 1).
- Read Mike's lines in the Cave of Oregano and Sugopolis, and the gym
  trainers' lines. Look for text that runs off the box or breaks in
  an odd place.


DEBUG MENU
----------
This build has the developer's debug menus turned on, so you can
skip ahead or set up a test:
  - Overworld: hold R and press START (mGBA keyboard: hold S, Enter)
  - In battle: press SELECT on the battle menu (mGBA: Backspace)
  - Sprite viewer: SELECT on a summary screen (mGBA: Backspace)
Use a separate save or a save state first, and say in your report
if that save used the debug menu.


AFTER-BATTLE GLITCHES
---------------------
If anything odd happens right after a battle (the player moving on
their own, buttons not working, a freeze, strange graphics), make a
SAVE STATE right away (mGBA: File > Save State) and send it in.


KNOWN PLACEHOLDERS
------------------
- Brian, Prof. Alan, Sam, Mike, Joe Q, WILDCARD, JASON and BLOBBY
  still use old Emerald sprites. Prof. Alan's battle sprite is a
  Gentleman.
- Retro Salvage Games shops still have blue roofs and MART signs.
- A few map graphics still say HOENN.
- Sam's shiny Metagross isn't cheddar-yellow yet.
- The new frames, chair and fireplace are first drafts of the art.

- Joe Q stuck in the wrong outfit, facing the wrong way, or

- Text that runs off the box
- Anything that blocks progress
- Say whether your save is a RANDOMIZER game, and whether it's new or
