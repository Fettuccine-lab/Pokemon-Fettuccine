POKEMON FETTUCCINE VERSION - PLAYTEST 8
=======================================

Thanks for testing! This is a big one: new starters, the Legends Z-A
Megas, new team changes, and a surprise on Route 101.

A NEW GAME is the best way to test this one, since the new starters,
the Route 101 battle and the PC item only appear in a new game.


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
1. You need a clean US Pokemon Emerald ROM (not included).
   It should match:
     CRC32: 1F1C08FB
     SHA-1: f3ae088181bf583e55daf962a92bb46f4f1d07b7
2. Apply fettuccine-playtest-8.bps to it. Either of these works:
     - Rom Patcher JS (in your browser): https://www.marcrobledo.com/RomPatcher.js/
     - Floating IPS (Flips)
3. Play the patched ROM in mGBA or any GBA emulator.

Your Playtest 7 save should keep working: give the new ROM the same
file name as before (so the save file matches) or copy the .sav across.
Use an in-game save, not a save state from an older playtest.

If you load an older save, two things to know:
- In a RANDOMIZER save, your wild Pokemon, trainers and gifts will
  change, because the list of possible Pokemon changed.
- If someone nearby looks like a fireplace or a chair right after
  loading, leave the area and come back. Please tell us if it happens.


NEW SINCE PLAYTEST 7
--------------------
New starters
- Alan's bag now has Rowlet, Chimchar and Sobble.
- Scorbunny and Froakie are now catchable: Scorbunny on Route 112,
  and Froakie by surfing.
- Prof. Alan's secret team now has Infernape and Inteleon.

Route 101
- The Pokemon chasing Prof. Alan is now a random legendary, different
  in each new game. It's still level 2, and the battle still can't
  be run from.

Legends Z-A Megas
- All the Z-A Mega Evolutions are in the game, and the Mega Stone Case
  holds their stones.
- Chikorita, Cyndaquil and Totodile now live on Route 117 (Totodile
  by surfing).
- After the Hall of Fame, look for Eternal Flower Floette on Route 104
  by the flower shop, and Magearna in the Abandoned Ship's Captain's
  Office.

Team changes
- FLANNERY: Mega Pyroar in every battle. Houndour is on her first team.
- WINONA: Mega Hawlucha in her second rematch.
- JUAN: Mega Golisopod in rematches 1 and 3.
- MIKE: Mega Falinks, and Vanilluxe replaces Gallade.
- SAM: Vileplume replaces Raichu.

Randomizer
- It now only uses Pokemon you can get in the game, so no more Paradox
  Pokemon or Ultra Beasts.
- The cursor now starts on NO at the New Game question.

Smaller changes
- Two new FRAME choices in Options: 21 is the Italian flag, 22 is pasta.
- Your bedroom PC now starts with TM15 Hyper Beam instead of a Potion.
- The clock's "Is this the correct time?" cursor now starts on YES.
- Prof. Alan's lab has a fireplace and a big comfy chair. Press A on
  them.
- Wonder Trade no longer gives out Paradox Pokemon.


PLEASE TRY
----------
- Start a few new games and look at the Route 101 legendary, both on
  the map and in battle. Try losing that battle too: the story should
  carry on as normal.
- Try FRAME 21 and 22 in dialogue, menus and the Start menu.
- Walk around Prof. Alan's chair and fireplace, before and after you
  get the Pokedex. You shouldn't be able to walk through them.
- Fight FLANNERY with each starter. Her level 29 Mega Pyroar is the
  change most likely to feel too hard.
- Mega Evolve some Z-A Pokemon, and scroll through the Mega Stone Case.
- Fight WINONA, JUAN, MIKE and SAM if you can, and check the right
  Pokemon Mega Evolves.
- If you can reach the post-game: catch a regular Floette first, then
  check the Eternal Flower Floette still shows up on Route 104.


DEBUG MENU
----------
This build has the developer's debug menus turned on, so you can
skip ahead or set up a test:
  - Overworld: hold R and press START (mGBA keyboard: hold S, Enter)
  - In battle: press SELECT on the battle menu (mGBA: Backspace)
  - Sprite viewer: SELECT on a summary screen (mGBA: Backspace)
Use a separate save or a save state first, and say in your report
if that save used the debug menu.


AFTER-BATTLE GLITCHES
---------------------
If anything odd happens right after a battle (the player moving on
their own, buttons not working, a freeze, strange graphics), make a
SAVE STATE right away (mGBA: File > Save State) and send it in.


KNOWN PLACEHOLDERS
------------------
- Brian, Prof. Alan, Sam, Mike, Joe Q, WILDCARD, JASON and BLOBBY
  still use old Emerald sprites. Prof. Alan's battle sprite is a
  Gentleman.
- Retro Salvage Games shops still have blue roofs and MART signs.
- A few map graphics still say HOENN.
- Sam's shiny Metagross isn't cheddar-yellow yet.
- The new frames, chair and fireplace are first drafts of the art.


PLEASE REPORT
-------------
- Crashes or freezes, especially in the Route 101 battle or when a Z-A
  Pokemon Mega Evolves
- Sprites that look wrong, especially on the Route 101 legendary, the
  lab furniture and the Z-A Megas
- Text that runs off the box
- Anything that blocks progress
- Battles that feel too hard or too easy with the new starters
- Say whether your save is a RANDOMIZER game, and whether it's new or
  from an older playtest

If you can, say where you were and what you were doing, and send a
screenshot or your save file.
